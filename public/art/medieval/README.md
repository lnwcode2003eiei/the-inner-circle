## Shared prompt

Original game illustration for The Inner Circle, unified realistic historical painting of European merchant life circa 1300. Grounded, lived-in, atmospheric, richly detailed but legible small. Natural muted brown timber, grey stone, aged gold, burgundy and navy fabrics, overcast daylight or candlelight. Human anatomy and believable tools. No modern buildings, electricity, suits, aircraft, machines, plastic, fantasy magic, shiny 3D miniatures, letters, logos, text or card borders. Main subject centered with safe margin for crop.
